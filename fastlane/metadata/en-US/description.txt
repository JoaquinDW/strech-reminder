Deep in work? Kip is too.

Unshrimp helps you make room for short stretching breaks during long desk days. A friendly shrimp companion nudges you to move, keeps you company through a guided routine and celebrates when you come back.

A SMALL BREAK THAT FITS YOUR DAY
Choose 2, 3 or 5 minutes of active movement. Follow illustrated video demonstrations, written cues and a timer through each move. Preparation and side changes add a few extra seconds. Pause, resume or skip a move whenever you need to.

REMINDERS ON YOUR SCHEDULE
Set your workdays, working hours and reminder interval. Start a break from a notification, snooze it for 10 minutes or open the app when you're ready. Notifications are optional.

CHOOSE WHERE TO FOCUS
Pick neck, shoulders or lower back as focus areas for your next break. Routines rotate the available moves so your breaks have some variety.

GIVE KIP'S DESK A LITTLE LIFE
Completed breaks earn energy that unlocks decorations for Kip's desk. Set a daily break goal, look back at your activity and move along the weekly Tide Pool Trail.

READY WITHOUT A CONNECTION
Demonstrations are included in the app, so routines work offline. No account is needed. Break history and preferences are saved on your iPhone. With Reduce Motion enabled, still poses replace automatic video playback.

Unshrimp is made for desk workers, remote workers and anyone who gets so absorbed in a screen that they forget to take a movement break.

Movement guidance is for general wellness and has not been professionally reviewed. Move gently and skip anything uncomfortable. Unshrimp does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
