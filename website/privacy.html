Privacy policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

Short version: Unshrimp doesn't collect your personal data. There's no account, no analytics and no advertising. What you do in the app stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores

To work, Unshrimp keeps a few things on your device:

  • Your preferences, such as workdays, working hours, reminder interval, break length and focus areas.
  • Your break history: when you took a break, how long it was and which areas it focused on.
  • Progress inside the app, such as energy earned, desk items unlocked and what Kip is wearing.

This information is stored only on your iPhone. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. It may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

Notifications

If you turn reminders on, Unshrimp asks for permission to send notifications. Reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone. No push server is involved and nothing about your schedule leaves the device. You can turn reminders off in the app or in iOS Settings at any time.

What the app doesn't do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or Health data.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because none is collected.

All exercise videos are bundled with the app, so routines work without an internet connection.

Deleting your data

Delete the app from your iPhone and all of its data is removed with it.

Children

Unshrimp is made for adults at work and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

This website

This site doesn't use cookies or analytics. Its fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads, as described in Google's privacy policy. If you email support, we use your message and address only to reply to you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If the app ever starts collecting data, the policy will say so before that version is released.
