Privacy policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

Short version: your progress and preferences stay on your iPhone. No account is needed. We use PostHog to understand app usage and RevenueCat to manage subscriptions. There is no advertising.

What the app stores

To work, Unshrimp keeps a few things on your device:

  • Your preferences, such as workdays, working hours, reminder interval, break length and focus areas.
  • Your break history: when you took a break, how long it was and which areas it focused on.
  • Progress inside the app, such as energy earned, desk items unlocked and what Kip is wearing.

Your detailed break history, work schedule and progress are stored on your iPhone and may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups. Some usage events and summary counts are sent as described below.

App analytics

We use PostHog (US Cloud) to understand onboarding, reminder interactions, completed or abandoned breaks, daily goals and weekly recap use, and to diagnose app errors. Events can include planned break length, daily goals, break counts and summary minutes. They are associated with a generated installation identifier and app/device metadata. Network requests also expose your IP address to the service.

We do not send your name, email address, work schedule or focus-area answers to analytics. Screen and tap autocapture, session replay and surveys are disabled. We do not use analytics for advertising.

Subscriptions and purchases

Apple processes payments through your App Store account. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases, restore subscriptions and determine whether you have access to Unshrimp Pro. RevenueCat receives purchase history, subscription status, a generated app user identifier and app/device information. We do not receive your payment card details.

Notifications

If you turn reminders on, Unshrimp asks for permission to send notifications. Reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone. No push server is involved and nothing about your schedule leaves the device. You can turn reminders off in the app or in iOS Settings at any time.

What the app doesn't do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising SDKs or cross-app advertising tracking.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or Health data.
  • No sale of your data. Service providers process the information described above to operate and improve the app.

All exercise videos are bundled with the app, so routines work without an internet connection.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its local progress and preferences. It does not cancel an App Store subscription or automatically delete records already held by our service providers. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple account settings. For questions or requests about data held by our service providers, contact us below; we may need information to locate the relevant records.

Children

Unshrimp is made for adults at work and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

This website

This site doesn't use cookies or analytics. Its fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads, as described in Google's privacy policy. If you email support, we use your message and address only to reply to you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
