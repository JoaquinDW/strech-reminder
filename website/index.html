How a break goes

Three minutes, start to finish.

Pick 2, 3 or 5 minutes. Each move has an illustrated video demo, a short written cue and a timer, so you can follow along without reading a wall of text.

  1. Kip looks up

    Your desk scene on the Home screen shows how many breaks you've taken today and when the next nudge is due.

  2. Follow the moves

    Watch the demo, read the cue, move with the countdown. Pause, replay or skip whenever you need to.

  3. Back to work

    Kip celebrates, you earn energy for the desk, and the day's progress bar fills in.

Choose where to focus

Neck, shoulders or lower back.

Pick one area or mix them. Routines rotate through the moves so the same stretch doesn't come up every time.

    Who is Unshrimp for?

    Desk workers, remote workers and anyone who gets so absorbed in a screen that they forget to get up. If you've ever looked up at 4 pm and realized you haven't moved since lunch, Kip gets it.

    Do I need to stand up or change clothes?

    No. Almost every move is done in your chair, in whatever you're wearing. March in place is the one standing move.

    Is this medical advice?

    No. Unshrimp is a general wellness app that helps you take movement breaks. It doesn't diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Move gently, skip anything that feels uncomfortable, and talk to a health professional if you have pain or an injury.

    Which iPhones does it run on?

    Any iPhone running iOS 18 or later.

    What happens if I ignore a reminder?

    Nothing bad. Kip never takes anything away, and the next reminder comes at your usual interval.
