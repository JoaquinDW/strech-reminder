Common questions

I'm not getting reminders

Open Settings › Notifications › Unshrimp and check that Allow Notifications is on. In the app, open the bell on the Home screen and make sure reminders are turned on and that today falls inside your workdays and hours. Focus modes can also hold notifications back.

Can I change how often Kip reminds me?

Yes. Open the bell on the Home screen and choose an interval of 45, 60, 90 or 120 minutes, plus the days and hours you work.

What does "In 10 minutes" do?

It snoozes that reminder. Kip asks again ten minutes later, once.

A move doesn't feel right for me

Skip it. Every move has a skip button, and you can choose which areas to focus on before each break. Unshrimp is a general wellness app and does not give medical advice. If you have pain or an injury, talk to a health professional.

Will I lose Kip's outfits or desk items if I skip days?

No. Nothing you've unlocked is ever taken away.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

Your break history is saved on your iPhone and comes along when you restore a new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. All break history and preferences are stored only on your iPhone and are removed with it.
